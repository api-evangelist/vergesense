New VergeSense has released a space metrics endpoint ( /metrics/spaces ) that allows users to retrieve metrics aggregated by a requested time interval. Previously, metrics available through the /metrics/hourly/spaces endpoint were always aggregated by hour. Users may now request metrics rolled up by shorter intervals, such as 10 or 15 minutes.
